Core USP

Don't just collect ratings. Gate the feedback journey.

Wonder QR places a feedback gate between the customer's rating and the configured public review journey. Lower ratings can be captured privately so the business can understand the issue and respond.

Higher ratings can continue to the configured public review path. The goal is a more useful customer conversation—not a promise of guaranteed positive reviews.

Scan

Customer scans the QR on the Wonder QR standee.

STEP 02

Rate

Customer gives a quick 1–5 star experience rating.

STEP 03

Gate

The configured feedback gate determines the next customer path.

The business can learn from private feedback and manage its public review journey.
