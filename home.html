Everything built into Wonder QR

A complete set of features already present in the platform—not just a QR generator.

⭐

Smart review journey

Customers rate 1–5 stars and follow the configured review path. Businesses can control the review threshold from the review settings.

💬

Private customer feedback

Lower ratings can open a private feedback form with message, optional name and optional contact number for management follow-up.

🔲

Branded review board

Every business gets a unique QR code and downloadable 4×6 review board/standee for counter, table, reception or display use.

📣

Get More Reviews sharing

Business owners can copy, share or send their unique customer review link and QR through WhatsApp and the device share menu.

📊

Business dashboard

View private feedback, filter feedback by all or today, manage business details, see QR information and access business links.

🔔

Business notifications

Push notifications can be sent to subscribed business admins, including notifications targeted to a specific business.

📱

Customer contact options

Businesses can configure phone, WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Pinterest, X and other supported links for customers.

🔐

Owner admin access

Business owners can access their dashboard using their 4-digit admin PIN without exposing protected setup information.

🧑‍💼

Sales & onboarding system

Wonder QR includes sales onboarding, business assignment, sales performance tracking, incentives and customer QR generation tools.

🏢

Super Admin management

Manage businesses, sales reps, assignments, business status, review settings and operational controls from the administration layer.

⚙️

Configurable review controls

Review filtering can be enabled or disabled, the minimum Google rating can be configured, and settings can be locked by administration.

📲

Mobile-first experience

The customer journey and business dashboard are designed for phone use, with installable app support and responsive layouts.

How Wonder QR works

Simple for the customer. Useful for the business.

01 — SCAN

Customer scans

The customer scans the business QR from the standee or shared link.

02 — RATE

Customer rates

A quick 1–5 star rating captures the experience.

03 — RESPOND

Review or feedback

The configured rating journey takes the customer to Google Reviews or private feedback.

04 — IMPROVE

Business acts

Management can review private feedback, contact details and customer-facing channels from the dashboard.
