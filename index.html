How Wonder QR works

Simple for the customer. Easy for the business.

01 — SCAN

Customer scans your QR

Place the Wonder QR standee or QR display where customers can easily see it.

02 — RATE

Customer selects a rating

The customer chooses from 1 to 5 stars based on their experience.

03 — RESPOND

Review or feedback

4–5 stars continue to Google. 1–3 stars are captured as private feedback for the business.
